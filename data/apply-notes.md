# Apply notes

Per-role warnings that `apply-sheet.mjs` renders onto the vault sheet, under the
row they belong to. User layer: nothing here is ever written automatically.

**Format:** one note per line, `<report#>: <text>`. Blank lines and `#` comments
are ignored. A note can wrap by repeating the number on the next line.

Use it for anything that must be read *before* sending, which the score cannot
express: an extra required artefact, an ambiguous eligibility clause, a
direction concern, or a reason to send one req ahead of another.
